Everything the game shows: the holograms, mannequins and shelves that servers decorate with now appear on the
cameras, and a round of fixes makes mobs, players and TNT the size and shape the game draws them.

## New

- **Display entities** (DisplayRenderer): block, item and text displays — the holograms, signs and decorations of
  servers — with their transformation interpolated like the game, billboards turning to the camera, brightness
  overrides, shadows, view range and teleport gliding. Text displays use the game's font with colours, bold,
  italic, underline and strikethrough, line wrapping, alignment, background and see-through text. The cameras'
  own markers stay hidden.
- **Shelves** (ShelfRenderer): the three items standing on a shelf, set on its middle by the size of their model,
  or on its bottom when the shelf is powered.
- **Mannequins**: drawn like players with the skin of their profile (a player's skin by name or id, the default
  skin, or the texture, model and cape of the profile's skin patch), their hidden skin layers and poses, and
  their description under the name.
- **Arrows and bee stingers stuck in players and mannequins** (StuckInBodyLayer), in the same places as in the
  game.
- **Sulfur cubes** (SulfurCubeRenderer): their real size, the small model of babies, the inner cube, the block
  they hold drawn inside them, and a primed cube swelling and flashing like TNT.

## Fixed

- Players were drawn a little too big: they now have the player renderer's scale.
- Slimes, magma cubes and sulfur cubes squash when they land and stretch when they jump; their shadows grow with
  their size.
- TNT about to explode swells like the game's (it grew too early) and flashes on the right ticks.
- Babies cast the smaller shadow of their age.
- A chest item showed the Christmas chest all year: item models that change with the date follow the viewer's
  clock.

## Updating

Replace the jar in `mods/` and restart the server. Configuration and cameras stay as they are.
