The game's background music with its Now Playing toast, and the music of The Immersive Music Mod when the server
has it.

## New

- **Now Playing toast** like the game's (NowPlayingToast): when a song of the background music starts, its title
  slides in from the top left on the game's own toast sprite, in the game's font, with the animated music notes
  changing colour, and slides out after five seconds. Settings > Now Playing toast turns it off.
- **The Immersive Music Mod** (TIMM): with the mod installed on the server, the viewer plays its music the way the
  mod does — each biome's and the End's own playlist, the song fading out a few seconds after the camera's biome
  stops having it, and the song of a structure (village, ancient city, stronghold, trial chambers...) the server
  finds around the camera, with the mod's song names on the toast. Its playlists and settings are read from
  `config/timm/` like the mod reads them; its songs come from the mod's jar and are not copied anywhere.
- **Assets of the server's mods** are used like the client's mod resource packs (their sounds, textures and
  texts), below the world's and the server's packs.
- The jukebox's rainbow "Now Playing" line is drawn in the game's font and GUI scale above where the hotbar
  would be.

## Fixes

- Name tags, signs and titles no longer keep a browser-drawn letter when the text was measured before the game's
  font had loaded.

## For administrators

- `modAssets: false` in `config/cctv/config.json` leaves the mods' assets out; `viewer.musicToast` (`on`, `off`)
  sets the toast's default for everybody.

## Updating

Replace the jar in `mods/` and restart the server. Configuration and cameras stay as they are.
