Block entities up close: CI now compares the viewer with Minecraft's own pictures of signs, chests and shulker boxes,
heads, banners, beds, bells and pots, and of armour stands, paintings and item frames at night, and the gaps they
showed are closed.

## New

- **Four more reference shots**, in a frozen world:
  - signs of every wood on a wall, hanging and standing turned three ways, with coloured, bold and glowing text
  - chests (single and double, trapped, ender, copper in each stage of oxidation) and shulker boxes of the colours
    facing every way
  - heads on a wall and on the floor, wall and standing banners with patterns, beds, bells on the floor and from the
    ceiling, an enchanting table, a conduit and a decorated pot with sherds
  - at night, by lanterns and torches: armour stands (plain, small, posed with a sword and a shield, without a base
    plate, in armour, turned away with an elytra), paintings and item frames with turned, invisible and glowing
    frames

  Forty-three shots now, each with a bound in CI.

## Fixed

- **Shulker boxes** facing a side were turned the wrong way (north for south, east for west).
- **Heads on a wall** faced the wall.
- **Mobs and armour stands that appear while the world is frozen** are as the game makes them until they tick:
  their bodies turned like their heads (an armour stand summoned facing away stood facing south) and an elytra
  hanging straight down.
- **Banners** sway by the world's game time like the game, so in a frozen world (`/tick freeze`) they stand still,
  and so do the clouds and the rain.

- **Decorated pots** in the reference shots get their sherds the 26.3 way (a map of the sides; the old list was
  not read).

## Updating

Replace the jar in `mods/` and restart the server. Configuration and cameras stay as they are.
