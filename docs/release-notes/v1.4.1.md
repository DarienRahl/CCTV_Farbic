More ways to look through a camera.

## New

- **Your own field of view** (Settings > Field of view): 30° to 110° like the game's option, or the camera's own.
  A wider one than the camera's makes the server send the wider view too.
- **Distance fog** (Settings > Distance fog): the game's fog, a smooth fade, atmospheric haze that thickens with
  distance, or minimal fog only at the very edge of the camera's range.
- **11 built-in post effects** (Settings > Post effect): cinematic (teal and orange, letterbox), film noir, thermal
  camera, night vision goggles, VHS tape, dome camera (fisheye), tilt-shift miniature, comic book outlines, retro
  pixels, dreamy glow and vivid. The server's own shaders in `config/cctv/shaders` are listed below them.
- **Paintings like the game draws them** (PaintingRenderer): the picture on a box 1/16 deep with the wooden back
  and edges, each block of a big painting lit by its own light. Paintings of data packs show their own pictures.
- **Music discs**: "Now Playing: …" appears in rainbow colours like in the game when a jukebox starts a disc, custom
  discs of data packs included (their sound and description come from the resource packs). A song that is already
  playing is heard from where it is when you open a camera or turn sounds on.
- **The world's own packs**: data packs with assets, the world's `resources.zip` and the server resource pack of
  `server.properties` are used like the game uses them, so custom paintings, discs, blocks, items and sounds need no
  copying. Unpacked pack folders work in `config/cctv/resourcepacks` too.
- **Mob animations checked against the game's models**: cats and ocelots sit, sneak, sprint, lie down next to
  sleeping players and relax like `AdultFelineModel` and `BabyFelineModel`; bees tuck their legs, only buzz in the air,
  bob the right way and roll over; chickens flap while falling; polar bears stand up; turtles walk on land (faster
  while digging, with the egg belly) and swim in water; dolphins, cod, salmon, tropical fish, tadpoles and pufferfish
  swim their own way (harder out of water); endermites and silverfish wriggle; vexes raise their swords when they
  charge; allays dance and spin to a jukebox and hold their item; striders walk and wave their bristles.

## Fixed

- The wings of chickens, bees, parrots, bats, allays, vexes and the ender dragon were posed like an elytra (floating
  above the body) since 1.3.0.

## For administrators

- New settings `worldResourcePacks` and `serverResourcePack` (both on); the order of the packs is in the README.

- `viewer.fog` and `viewer.fov` in `config/cctv/config.json` set the defaults for everybody; `viewer.postShader`
  also takes the built-in effects (`"builtin:cinematic"`).
- The stream takes `?fov=` (30 to 110): the camera's cone of sections is made for the widest field of view of its
  viewers.

## Updating

Replace the jar in `mods/` and restart the server. Configuration and cameras stay as they are.
