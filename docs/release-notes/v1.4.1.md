More ways to look through a camera.

## New

- **Your own field of view** (Settings > Field of view): 30° to 110° like the game's option, or the camera's own.
  A wider one than the camera's makes the server send the wider view too.
- **Distance fog** (Settings > Distance fog): the game's fog, a smooth fade, atmospheric haze that thickens with
  distance, or minimal fog only at the very edge of the camera's range.
- **11 built-in post effects** (Settings > Post effect): cinematic (teal and orange, letterbox), film noir, thermal
  camera, night vision goggles, VHS tape, dome camera (fisheye), tilt-shift miniature, comic book outlines, retro
  pixels, dreamy glow and vivid. The server's own shaders in `config/cctv/shaders` are listed below them.

## For administrators

- `viewer.fog` and `viewer.fov` in `config/cctv/config.json` set the defaults for everybody; `viewer.postShader`
  also takes the built-in effects (`"builtin:cinematic"`).
- The stream takes `?fov=` (30 to 110): the camera's cone of sections is made for the widest field of view of its
  viewers.

## Updating

Replace the jar in `mods/` and restart the server. Configuration and cameras stay as they are.
