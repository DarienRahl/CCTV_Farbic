The first part of 1.4 (performance) of the
[roadmap](https://github.com/DarienRahl/CCTV_Farbic/blob/v1.4.0/docs/ROADMAP.md).

## New

- **Sections are kept in the browser** (IndexedDB). Reopening a camera, or opening another one in the same
  dimension, downloads only the sections that changed since: the browser says which sections it has
  (with a hash of each) and the server confirms the unchanged ones in a few bytes instead of sending them
  again. In CI the second visit gets every one of its 70 sections from the cache. `?cache=0` in the camera
  link turns it off; the cache keeps at most 60 000 sections.
- **Less server memory per camera**: sections far from the camera are kept only as the message the
  viewers get (a few KB instead of about 25 KB each) and read back from it when something in them changes.
- **Budgets in CI**: the server's time per camera tick, the size of section messages and the page's time per
  frame are measured on every change and must stay within limits. `/api/status` shows the tick time and
  the sections sent, kept from browsers' caches and compacted.

- **Cameras under water, in lava or in powder snow** look like the game from there: the underwater overlay
  (moving with the view, as bright as the light at the camera) and the thick red fog of lava or the white
  fog of powder snow.

- **New mobs of newer game versions** appear with their real model and texture (found by the game's
  naming) and a simple walk instead of a plain box, before the viewer learns their exact details.

## For developers

- `?cache=1` on the stream adds `vid` and `epoch` to `init`; `POST /api/cameras/{name}/cache?vid=&epoch=`
  takes the cached sections as `x,y,z,hash,...` (cyrb53 of the section message, `Protocol.hash53`), and
  the new `keep` event lists the ones the viewer can show from its cache.

## Updating

Replace the jar in `mods/` and restart the server. Configuration and cameras stay as they are.
