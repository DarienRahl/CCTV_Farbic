A big update of the viewer and the server. The camera picture looks like Minecraft 26.3 (also at
night, in the rain and in the End), every mob has its real model and the view distance can reach
1024 blocks.

## New

- **Camera at eye height.** `/cctv create <name>` places the camera where your eyes are. `~ ~ ~`
  coordinates are also relative to your eye height.
- **Every mob with its game model.** The geometry comes from `client.jar` (happy ghast, horses,
  donkeys, mules, llamas, wolves, cats, villagers with professions, cold and warm variants,
  babies…). Saddles, armour, collars, sheep wool, harnesses, glowing eyes and walk, head and arm
  animations are drawn. No more white boxes.
- **Huge view distance (like the Bobby mod).** Terrain in unloaded chunks is read from the world
  files without loading chunks. `maxRange` goes up to 1024 blocks (`farTerrain` option).
- **No more stutter.** Block meshes are built in Web Workers, distant sections are merged into
  bigger buffers and animated textures no longer rebuild the whole atlas.
- **Lighter on the server's main thread.** The server only copies section data; decoding,
  compression, JSON and reading world files happen on separate threads (`workerThreads`).
- **Light like 26.3.** The lightmap, fog, sky and fog colours are computed from the same
  environment attributes (dimension, biome, time of day, weather) as in the game.
- **Rain, snow and thunderstorms.** Precipitation columns like in the game (they stop at roofs,
  snow in cold biomes), lightning bolts and sky flashes, a darker sky and fog in the rain.
- **The End.** The End sky and **End flashes** (in sync with the server), including the purple
  lighting of the world.
- **Names only for visible entities.** Player names and mob labels only show when the entity is on
  screen and not hidden behind blocks.
- **Shaders.** A "Shaders" graphics mode: shadows from the sun and the moon, waving leaves and
  grass, water with waves and reflections, glow and sun rays. Four quality levels.
- **Custom effects and skies.** GLSL post shaders (`config/cctv/shaders/`, examples included) and
  sky boxes (`config/cctv/skyboxes/`: six faces or a panorama, a `sunset` example included).
- **Settings for every viewer.** The `viewer` section in `config.json` sets the default graphics,
  shader, a sky box per dimension, clouds, mode and labels. `lockSettings` stops viewers from
  changing them, `/cctv reload` applies changes without a restart.
- Chests, shulker boxes, heads, banners, bells and decorated pots, paintings, item frames with
  items, 3D items on the ground and in hands, boats, minecarts, arrows and tridents.

## Updating from 1.0.0

Replace the jar in `mods/`. The config gets the new keys; new cameras default to a range of 96
blocks and `maxRange` is 512. Existing cameras keep their settings.

## Limitations

- Mob animations that use keyframes in the game (sniffer, warden, frog…) are simplified.
- Banner patterns, particles and sounds are not drawn.
- Distant terrain shows the state of the last world save.

The full description, configuration and API are in the README of the repository.
