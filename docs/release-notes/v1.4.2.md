More of the game's options and sounds.

## New

- **Background music** like the game's MusicManager once sounds are on: the music of the place the camera is in
  (each biome's and dimension's own, the underwater music with the camera in water, the boss music during the
  dragon fight), the first song after a few seconds and the next ones after the game's pauses.
  Settings > Music: off, default, frequent or constant (the game's Music Frequency option).
- **Items drawn from the game's item model definitions** (`items/*.json`, ItemModelResolver) in hands, on the
  ground and in item frames: dyed leather armour, coloured potions, splash potions and tipped arrows, loaded
  crossbows, firework stars, trimmed armour, clocks showing the time, and every layer
  of layered items. Items of later versions and of resource packs follow their definitions without changes.
- **Occlusion culling like the game** (VisGraph and SectionOcclusionGraph): sections the camera cannot see into
  behind hills, walls and floors are not drawn, about a third fewer in a hilly view. Far regions, which are merged
  into one buffer each, are culled section by section too (frustum, render distance and the occlusion graph), so
  another quarter fewer quads are drawn at large view distances. `?occlusion=0` in a camera link turns both off
  for comparisons.
- **Fluids in every case like the game**: water next to slabs, stairs and other partial blocks, and water inside
  waterlogged blocks, hides exactly the faces FluidRenderer hides; the flowing texture turns like the game's around
  signs, pressure plates and banners (`#blocks_fluid_flow`).
- **Entities skinned on the GPU**: models stay on the graphics card and only one matrix per model part is sent
  each frame, about a third of the data per frame with a few mobs in view and much less with many; `?skinning=0`
  poses them on the CPU as before.
- **⏺ Record and ⏱ Timelapse** in the viewer: a video of the camera with its sounds, or a timelapse (one picture
  every second to five minutes, saved as a video when stopped), with the camera's name, place, date and game time
  burnt in like a CCTV recording. Made in the viewer's browser; `recording: false` in the config hides them.
- **Biome blend** (Settings > Biome blend): off, 3x3 up to 15x15 like the game's option; 5x5 stays the default.

## For administrators

- `viewer.music` (`off`, `default`, `frequent`, `constant`) and `viewer.biomeBlend` (0 to 7) in
  `config/cctv/config.json` set the defaults for everybody.

## Updating

Replace the jar in `mods/` and restart the server. Configuration and cameras stay as they are.
