Mobs dressed up: CI now compares the viewer with Minecraft's own pictures of mobs in armour and tack and in their
moods, and the gaps they showed are closed.

## New

- **Eight more reference shots**, in a frozen world:
  - zombies and others in armour of every material (dyed leather, chainmail, iron, gold, diamond, netherite and
    copper) with trims, a turtle shell, a baby in diamond, a piglin in gold with a crossbow, a husk with a carved
    pumpkin on its head, a skeleton with a sword and a shield
  - horses in leather (dyed), iron, golden, diamond, copper and netherite armour, some saddled, a donkey and a mule
    with chests, a llama with a carpet and a chest, a trader llama
  - saddled pigs, striders, camels and camel husks, undead horses in armour, nautiluses in armour
  - happy ghasts in harnesses, and a young one
  - tamed wolves and cats with collars, sitting or in armour (dyed and worn down), an angry wolf, foxes asleep,
    sitting and crouching
  - parrots, tropical fish and axolotls of their colours
  - a shorn sheep, a charged creeper, a goat with one horn, a snow golem without its pumpkin, a cracked iron golem,
    Dinnerbone and Toast, shulkers
  - pandas of every gene

  Fifty-nine shots now, each with a bound in CI.

## Fixed

- **Happy ghasts in a harness** wear their goggles pushed up unless someone rides them (they hung over the eyes),
  and their body is squeezed a little into the harness like the game's.
- **A goat that lost a horn** shows only the one it has.
- **Shorn sheep** show their dyed undercoat (a shorn red sheep was bare).
- **Baby armour** sits where the game puts it (the leggings slipped off one leg): every layer now follows its
  parent model by how far the parts moved from their rest pose, as each layer model starts from its own.
- **Energy swirls** of charged creepers and withers are brighter, close to the game's: drawn on both sides and
  writing depth like the game's pipeline.
- **Skeletons** hold their items a pixel further out than their thin arms, and **babies** hold theirs closer, in
  their smaller hands.
- **Faces seen from behind** (a strider's bristles on its far side, flat fins and wings) are lit as the game lights
  them: from the other side.
- **Warm zombie nautiluses in armour** no longer show their corals through it.

## Updating

Replace the jar in `mods/` and restart the server. Configuration and cameras stay as they are.
