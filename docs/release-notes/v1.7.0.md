The world around the camera: the world border's force field, stacks on the ground, lingering potion and
dragon's breath clouds, the wither's armour and minecarts that ride their rails like in the game.

## New

- **The world border**: the scrolling force field on its walls as the camera comes near, blue while it stands,
  green while it grows and red while it shrinks, following the border as it moves.
- **Stacks on the ground**: bigger stacks show up to five copies scattered like in the game, flat items stacked
  front to back.
- **Lingering potion and dragon's breath clouds** puff their particles in the potion's colour (and the dragon's
  purple) over the cloud.
- **The wither's armour** at half health, and the charged creeper's aura in the game's grey, scrolling like in
  the game; an invisible charged creeper still shows its aura.
- **Minecarts** sit on their rail and tilt along slopes, rock when hit, and carry their block at its height: lit
  furnaces, blocks set by commands, and TNT that swells and flashes on its fuse.

## Fixed

- Dropped items rest 1/16 above the ground by the real size of their model, like in 26.3.

## Updating

Replace the jar in `mods/` and restart the server. Configuration and cameras stay as they are.
