More of the picture now matches the game: beacons shine, end portals shimmer with the game's own
shader, players wear their capes and elytra, and burning mobs burn. The next part of the
[roadmap](https://github.com/DarienRahl/CCTV_Farbic/blob/v1.3.0/docs/ROADMAP.md) (1.3, a 1:1 picture).

## New

- **Beacon beams**, like BeaconRenderer: every colour section (stained glass above the beacon),
  the turning beam and its glow, reaching the sky and drawn as far as the view goes. The beam
  shows up when the beacon turns on and changes when glass is added, like in the game.
- **End portals and end gateways** with the game's own `rendertype_end_portal` shader: the
  layered, drifting star field instead of a flat box.
- **Capes** from the player's Mojang profile, swinging as they walk, run and fall like the game's
  cape; **elytra** on players and armoured mobs, spreading while flying and folding when
  sneaking. A player who shows their cape wears it on the elytra too.
- **Skin layers:** the parts of the skin a player turned off (hat, jacket, sleeves, trousers,
  cape) are hidden like in the game.
- **Entity shadows like the game's:** the round shadow texture on the tops of the blocks under a
  mob, spreading over block edges and steps, fainter the deeper it falls and in the dark, and fading
  out 16 blocks from the camera like in the game.
- **Enchantment glint** on enchanted weapons, tools and armour, held, lying on the ground or in
  item frames, and on elytra, moving like the game's glint.
- **Burning entities are wrapped in flames**, invisible ones too.
- **Invisible mobs** show their armour, saddles and held items like in the game, and cast no
  shadow.
- **Food cooking on campfires.**
- **Books on enchanting tables and lecterns.** The enchanting table's book floats over the
  table, turns to the nearest player, opens and flips its pages, and closes and slowly spins when
  nobody is near, like EnchantTableRenderer.

## Fixed

- Chests, banners, decorated pots and mob heads were drawn as plain coloured boxes on 26.3 servers
  (these blocks now have an empty block model); they show their models again.

## For developers

- CI builds a beacon, an end portal, a campfire with food, an enchanting table, a lectern and an
  enchanted helmet in the test scene and checks their data. The viewer screenshots now fail on any script error and on
  a mob drawn as a plain box (a model or texture the viewer could not find), naming it.
- New server endpoints `GET /cape/{uuid}?name=` (the player's cape, cached with the skin) and
  `GET /assets/misc/{path}.png` (textures of `textures/misc`).
- The screenshot step also fails on blocks drawn as plain boxes.

## Updating

Replace the jar in `mods/` and restart the server. Configuration and cameras stay as they are.
