Every mob up close: CI now compares the viewer with Minecraft's own pictures of every kind of mob, seen from a few
blocks in a frozen world, and the gaps they showed are closed.

## New

- **Thirteen rows of mobs in the reference renders**: farm animals, pets, the undead, illagers, the Nether's, small
  ones, flyers, fish and squid, big ones, mounts, rare ones, sea creatures and ghasts, each row seen from a few
  blocks. The world is frozen first (`/tick freeze`), so the game and the viewer show the same pose. Twenty-seven
  shots now, each with a bound in CI.
- **The breeze's wind**: a breeze is wrapped in its whirl of wind, its texture drifting with the breeze's age, like
  the game's BreezeWindLayer.
- **Copper golem statues**: placed statues are drawn in their pose, facing and oxidation (they were missing), and a
  statue in an item frame or a hand stands upright (it was upside down).

## Fixed

- **Models drawn from both sides**: like 26.3, mob models are drawn from both sides (a chicken's thin legs show
  through the clear front of their boxes); only the bat and baby turtles hide their back faces.
- **Held items like the game**: a mob's arm stays where it is whatever it holds; only a spear, an aggressive
  drowned's trident and an aggressive skeleton's bow pose it (an aggressive skeleton without a bow raises its arms).
  A drowned's trident is the trident model upright in its hand, not a flat picture far above its head.
- **Piglins**: their ears flap as they walk, and they dance, hold their weapon high, aim crossbows and admire gold
  like in the game.
- **Wither skeletons** cast the shadow of a skeleton.
- **Item models moved by their definition**: a transformation on a select or condition of an item definition moves
  the special model below it (the trident's and the copper golem statue's flip).
- **Glowing eyes**: an enderman's eyes are purple, not white. Eyes are blended over the model like the game does
  in 26.3, not added to it.
- **Guardians** start with their spikes drawn in, like a guardian that has not moved yet.
- **Frozen worlds** (`/tick freeze`): the freeze reaches the viewer before anything spawned after it, and nothing
  moves on its own any more: no particles, block animations, ambient sounds, bat wings or warden heartbeats, like in
  the game.
- **The camera's own player**: the player standing at a camera's eyes is no longer drawn around the camera (under
  water their head filled the picture).

## Updating

Replace the jar in `mods/` and restart the server. Configuration and cameras stay as they are.
