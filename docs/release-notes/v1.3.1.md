Sounds, the game's particles for what happens in the world, opening chests, armour trims, shields and
maps in item frames. The next part of the
[roadmap](https://github.com/DarienRahl/CCTV_Farbic/blob/v1.3.1/docs/ROADMAP.md) (1.3, a 1:1 picture).

## New

- **Sounds.** The 🔈 button in the viewer plays what a player standing at the camera would hear: the
  sounds the server sends (mobs, steps, doors, chests, explosions, note blocks, jukebox songs) and the
  ones the game client makes for level events (blocks breaking, dispensers, anvils...). They come from
  the right direction and fade with distance like in the game's sound engine, and the sound variant is
  the same one the game picks. The server downloads each sound from Mojang the first time it is played
  and keeps it (config `sounds`, on by default).
- **Particles from the server, like the game client makes them:** pieces of broken blocks in the
  block's own texture, explosions with their flash and smoke, the particles the server sends
  (crits, sword sweeps, hearts, dust, spells...), dispenser smoke, bone meal sparkles, lava fizz,
  the poof when a mob dies or spawns, love and taming hearts, villager hearts, anger and happiness,
  and the swirls of potion effects on players and mobs.
- **Armour like the game draws it:** the layers come from the game's own equipment files, so
  leather armour shows its dye, **armour trims** show in their material's colours (iron trims on iron
  armour in the darker iron, like in the game), and horses, zombie and skeleton horses, wolves,
  llamas, nautiluses and happy ghasts wear their body armour, carpets and harnesses the same way.
- **Cracks on blocks being mined**, like the game draws them for other players' digging.
- **Mobs in spawners**: the spawner's mob spins inside the cage while the camera is near, with the
  spawner's smoke and flames; trial spawners show theirs while waiting for players or active.
- **Shields** are drawn with their base colour and banner patterns, in hands, on the ground and in
  item frames.
- **Chests, ender chests and shulker boxes open and close** when players use them, **bells swing**
  when rung and **note blocks show their notes**, from the block events the server sends players.
- **Maps in item frames** show their picture (and the banner markers and other decorations shown on
  frames), updated as the map changes.

## Fixed

- Item frames were drawn back to front (the item on the wall side); they now face the room like in
  the game, and invisible frames show their item or map.
- Items held in the left hand were placed like right-hand items; they are mirrored like in the game.
  Held items now use the display transforms of the game's item models.
- Old 64x32 player skins (like Notch's) showed a black box around the head on player heads and
  players; they are converted like the game does.

## For developers

- Entity frames carry the effects since the last frame ("fx"): level events, particle packets,
  explosions and entity events that make particles. CI breaks a block, blows up TNT and kills sheep
  and checks they arrive.
- Sounds travel with the effects ("s": at a position, "se": following an entity, "sg": global,
  "js"/"jx": jukebox songs). New endpoints `GET /assets/sounds.json` (the game's sound events merged
  with resource packs) and `GET /assets/sound/{namespace}/{path}.ogg`. CI checks both and that the note
  block's, the broken block's and the explosion's sounds arrive.
- Block events travel with the effects ("be": position, block, the two event numbers); CI plays a
  note block with redstone and checks it arrives.
- Entity frames carry the blocks being broken ("bp"); spawners and trial spawners are block entities
  with their mob ("k": "spawner"). CI puts a zombie spawner into the scene.
- Entities carry what the equipment layers need ("eq": equipment asset, dye, trim); item frames with a
  map carry its id and picture version. New endpoints `GET /map/{id}` (the picture, 128 x 128 RGBA)
  and `GET /assets/{trims|palettes|map}/...` (trim patterns and palettes, map decorations).

## Updating

Replace the jar in `mods/` and restart the server. Configuration and cameras stay as they are.
