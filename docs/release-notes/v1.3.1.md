The game's particles for what happens in the world. The next part of the
[roadmap](https://github.com/DarienRahl/CCTV_Farbic/blob/v1.3.1/docs/ROADMAP.md) (1.3, a 1:1 picture).

## New

- **Particles from the server, like the game client makes them:** pieces of broken blocks in the
  block's own texture, explosions with their flash and smoke, the particles the server sends
  (crits, sword sweeps, hearts, dust, spells...), dispenser smoke, bone meal sparkles, lava fizz,
  the poof when a mob dies or spawns, love and taming hearts, villager hearts, anger and happiness,
  and the swirls of potion effects on players and mobs.

## For developers

- Entity frames carry the effects since the last frame ("fx"): level events, particle packets,
  explosions and entity events that make particles. CI breaks a block, blows up TNT and kills sheep
  and checks they arrive.

## Updating

Replace the jar in `mods/` and restart the server. Configuration and cameras stay as they are.
