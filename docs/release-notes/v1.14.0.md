The game as the judge, everywhere: CI now compares the viewer with Minecraft's own pictures of eleven scenes, from a
sunset to the End, and the gaps the new ones showed are closed.

## New

- **Six more reference shots**: dusk towards the setting sun, the day view in the rain, a snowy plain while it
  snows, a cave lit only by blocks, a room in the Nether and a platform in the End join day, night, mobs, a room and
  under water. Every shot has a bound in CI; all are within 1 % of the game (the ones without rain or snow within
  0.6 %).

## Fixed

- **Sunsets without wedges**: with a camera looking exactly north, south, east or west, the sunset glow showed two
  sharp wedges beside the sun on software-rendered browsers (the ends of the sunrise fan lay exactly beside the
  camera and their triangles were dropped).
- **Snow golems look at you**: the carved face of a snow golem's pumpkin was on the side of its head.
- **End flashes stop with the clock**: when the world's time is stopped (`/gamerule advance_time false`) the End's
  flashes stand still in the viewer like in the game.

## Updating

Replace the jar in `mods/` and restart the server. Configuration and cameras stay as they are.
