Conduits, end gateway beams, suspicious sand, fishing lines, cracked wolf armour and 3D block items. The next part of the
[roadmap](https://github.com/DarienRahl/CCTV_Farbic/blob/v1.3.3/docs/ROADMAP.md) (1.3, a 1:1 picture).

## New

- **Conduits** are drawn like in the game: a still shell, or once a prismarine frame surrounds it
  under water, the turning cage, the swirling wind and the eye that follows the camera (open while
  it hunts). Like the game client, the viewer checks the water and the frame around it itself.
- **End gateways** flare up with their purple beam when something goes through.
- **Suspicious sand and gravel** show the item coming out of the side being brushed.
- **Fishing**: the hook faces the camera and the black line sags from it to the hand holding the rod,
  like FishingHookRenderer.
- **Wolf armour** shows its cracks as it wears down (low, medium, high, as in WolfArmorLayer).
- **Chests, shulker boxes, heads, banners, conduits, decorated pots, copper golem statues and beds as
  items** (dropped, held, in item frames) are drawn with their real models instead of plain boxes. The
  server reads which items need them from the game's own item definitions, so new ones come with updates.

## For developers

- Block states list whether they belong to a conduit's frame ("tg": minecraft:conduit_effect_block);
  suspicious sand and gravel being brushed are block entities with their item and side ("k": "brush").
  Fishing hooks name their player and the rod's arm ("fish"), a wolf's body armour its cracks ("eq"
  "k"). The asset bundle carries the special and composite models of the item definitions
  ("specialItems"). CI brushes suspicious sand, places a conduit and summons a wolf in worn armour.

## Updating

Replace the jar in `mods/` and restart the server. Configuration and cameras stay as they are.
