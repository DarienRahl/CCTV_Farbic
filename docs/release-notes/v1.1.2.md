Fixes for holes in the terrain, players without animations and a few rendering errors, and name
tags that look exactly like in the game.

## New

- **Name tags 1:1 like in Minecraft.** Names above heads are drawn with the game's own font (taken
  from `client.jar`, with the same glyph widths), in the world at the game's scale, half a block
  above the head, with the translucent background, the half-strength text that shows through blocks,
  lighting from the lightmap and dimmed tags for sneaking players. They follow the game's rules:
  players within 64 blocks (32 when sneaking) and mobs whose custom name is set to always show.
  "Mob labels" still adds tags for every other mob.

## Fixed

- **Holes in cliffs, mountains and hillsides far from the camera.** Far sections that lay below the
  lowest surface point of their own chunk were skipped as "buried", even when their side was visible
  from a lower chunk in front of them. Now a section is only skipped when it is also below the
  surface of the neighbouring chunks towards the camera. Cameras under the ground (caves, cellars)
  no longer skip anything.
- **Chunks disappearing and loading again on slow connections.** The server pushed sections faster
  than a slow connection could take them; when the queue overflowed the viewer was dropped, reconnected
  and started loading from the beginning. Sections are now sent at the speed of the connection.
- **Turning the view showed the edge of the world.** Dragging the view only sees terrain that the
  server streams around the camera's direction. The streamed area is a bit wider now and the view
  stops turning at its edge.
- **Players had no animations.** The server does not animate players (their game client does), so
  they stood still while moving. The viewer now computes walking, arm swinging and the body turning
  towards the movement the way the game client does for other players, plus sneaking, swimming,
  crawling and gliding with elytra. Mobs ridden by players (horses, pigs, camels…) walk too.
- **Players drawn as grey boxes.** When a skin could not be loaded (offline-mode servers, no access
  to Mojang) the fallback texture was never used. Players now get the game's default skin for their
  UUID (Steve, Alex, Zuri…).
- **A single broken block model no longer removes its whole 16×16×16 section** (it is drawn as a
  plain box and reported in the browser console instead).
- **Hollow trees in the distance.** Far away, leaves hide each other's inner faces; they are now
  drawn solid there, like the game's "fast" leaves, instead of showing see-through holes.
- Grass and flowers in the camera's own block are not drawn (a camera placed while standing in tall
  grass showed a screen full of grass).

## Updating

Replace the jar in `mods/` and restart the server. Configuration and cameras stay as they are.

## Known issue

A camera that nobody watched for a while can stop streaming to new viewers until the server
restarts (the page connects but stays empty). This is being fixed in the next version.
