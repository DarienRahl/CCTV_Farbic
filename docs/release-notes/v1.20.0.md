Every kind of mob: CI now compares the viewer with Minecraft's own pictures of young mobs and of the kinds of wolves,
farm animals, villagers and horses, and the gaps they showed are closed.

## New

- **Eight more reference shots**, in a frozen world at noon:
  - the young of the farm (a calf, a piglet, a lamb, a chick, a mooshroom calf, a kit, a kid, a cria and a turtle
    hatchling), of the wild (a pup, a kitten, a fox kit, an ocelot, a polar bear, a panda, an armadillo, a bee and an
    axolotl) and of the folk (a villager, zombies, a husk, a drowned, a zombie villager, piglins, a hoglin and a
    strider), and young horses, donkeys, mules, camels, sniffers and skeleton horses
  - every kind of wolf
  - frogs, chickens, pigs and cows of each climate
  - villagers of each biome, profession and level, and a zombie villager
  - horses of each colour and markings, llamas and a trader llama

  Fifty-one shots now, each with a bound in CI.

## Fixed

- **Villagers' hats** are drawn like the game draws them: a villager wears its biome's hat or its profession's,
  as the textures' metadata says (a swamp fisherman wore the swamp's leaves over its fishing hat). The server now
  serves those metadata files to the viewer, including a resource pack's.
- **Horse markings** blend with the coat like the game's: their half-clear pixels were drawn solid white.

## Updating

Replace the jar in `mods/` and restart the server. Configuration and cameras stay as they are.
