Mobs move like in the game, blocks smoke, burn and drop leaves, and banners, pots and heads show
their details. The next part of the [roadmap](https://github.com/DarienRahl/CCTV_Farbic/blob/v1.2.0/docs/ROADMAP.md)
(1.2 easy updates and the game's animations, 1.3 a 1:1 picture).

## New

- **Keyframe animations from the game.** The sniffer, warden, frog, camel, armadillo, bat, breeze,
  creaking, rabbit, copper golem, nautilus and baby axolotl play the game's own animation
  definitions, read from `client.jar` (sniffing and digging, the warden's roar and sonic boom,
  croaking, the camel sitting down and dashing, the armadillo rolling up...). The server sends
  which animations are running and the entity events, so they start when they start in the game.
  New or changed animations of a game version come along without code changes.
- **Animations driven by entity events:** iron golem, ravager, hoglin and zoglin attacks, the
  ravager's stun and roar, sheep eating grass, wolves shaking off water and tilting their head
  when begging, goats lowering their head to ram. Goats lose their horns like in the game and a
  wolf's tail follows its health.
- **Particles** like the game's: torch, candle and campfire flames and smoke, campfire smoke
  columns, lava pops and drips, water drips, smoke from fire, furnaces, end rods, nether portals,
  spore blossoms, crying obsidian, fireflies and falling leaves, with the game's sprites, physics,
  light and fog. They can be turned off in **⚙ Settings**, or for everyone with
  `viewer.particles: false`.
- **Banners with their patterns** (every layer in its colour, the flag swaying), **decorated pots
  with their sherds** and **player heads with the owner's skin**. Placing or breaking them shows
  up right away.
- **Mob names from the game's language file.** Mob labels say "Zombie Villager" instead of
  `zombie_villager`. The new `language` setting (`pl_pl`, `de_de`...) picks another game
  language: its file comes from a resource pack or is downloaded once from Mojang, like the
  launcher does.

## Improved

- **Video wall:** tiles that are scrolled out of view or in a hidden tab stop drawing, tiles draw
  at most 30 frames per second (20 when small) and at one pixel per CSS pixel. The stream keeps
  running, so a tile is current the moment it shows again.
- Model poses that were ported from older versions now match 26.3: sitting wolves, ravager legs,
  hoglin ears and head, the iron golem's sway while walking.

## For developers

- The CI server test also checks animation definitions, running animation states, entity events,
  mob names, banner patterns, pot sherds, player heads and that particles are drawn.
- `docs/ported-classes.txt` lists the new ported classes (animations, particles, block animate
  ticks, event-driven models), so `inspect-minecraft` with `compare_with` shows their changes in a
  new game version.

## Updating

Replace the jar in `mods/` and restart the server. Configuration and cameras stay as they are; the
asset cache is rebuilt once (the entity model file now includes the animations).
