Every particle of the game: all of 26.3's particle types, the particles of level and entity events, the
Nether's ash and spores, and the blocks' own particles, from glyphs flying into enchanting tables to vaults
calling their players.

## New

- **Every particle type of 26.3**: ash, white ash, crimson and warped spores, souls, sculk charges and shrieks,
  vibrations flying to their listener, sonic booms, glow squids' glow and ink, wax on and off, scrapes,
  electric sparks, enchanting glyphs, gusts, dust plumes, dust changing colour, falling dust, snowflakes,
  spit, trails, trial spawner flames, fishing wakes, sneezes and more, with the game's physics, turned and
  lit like in the game.
- **Level events**: splash and lingering potions bursting in their colour, dragon fireballs, eyes of ender
  breaking, teleporting dragon eggs and endermen, waxing and scraping copper, sculk spreading, a mace's
  smash, other players mining blocks, composters, trial spawners spawning, detecting and ejecting.
- **Entity events**: a totem of undying saving someone, mobs teleporting, witches drinking.
- **Biome ambience**: the Nether's ash, white ash and spores around the camera, and any data pack's ambient
  particles.
- **Blocks' own particles**: enchanting tables' glyphs flying from their bookshelves, ender chests' and end
  portals' specks, redstone wire, repeaters and lit ores glowing, dust falling under sand, gravel and
  concrete powder, mycelium, brewing stands, wet sponges, active sculk sensors, conduits, lightning rods
  sparking in thunderstorms, trial spawners and vaults — which now call the players they are waiting for,
  with sparks flying to their keyhole, and burn only while they show an item — and bees dripping nectar.

## Updating

Replace the jar in `mods/` and restart the server. Configuration and cameras stay as they are.
