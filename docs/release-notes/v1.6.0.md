Players and what they carry: parrots on shoulders, hats and heads, bows drawn and shields raised, left-handed
players and mobs, and name tags with their team colours and scores.

## New

- **Parrots on players' shoulders**, sitting there and looking where the player looks.
- **Heads and hats** (CustomHeadLayer): skulls and player heads with their owner's skin, carved pumpkins,
  banners and data packs' hats worn by players, mannequins, armour stands, zombies, skeletons, villagers,
  illagers, piglins, copper golems and sulfur cubes, each at its renderer's place and size.
- **Items in use**: players and mannequins draw bows, charge and aim crossbows, raise shields, look through
  spyglasses, blow goat horns, brush and throw tridents with the game's arm poses; a swing moves the arms and the
  body like in the game.
- **Left-handed players and mobs** hold their main item in the left hand and swing with it.
- **Name tags like the game's**: team colours, prefixes and suffixes and coloured custom names, the scoreboard's
  below_name objective under players' names (like health or kills), teams that hide name tags, no tag on a
  ridden mob, and the name_tag_distance and below_name_distance attributes.
- A riptide trident's spin with its whirls, and deadmau5's ears.
- Mobs frozen in powder snow, zombies, piglins and hoglins turning into something else, skeletons becoming strays
  and cold striders shake like in the game.

## Fixed

- Shields without patterns were not drawn at all (26.3 renamed their textures).
- Sleepers lie along their bed; spiders, silverfish and endermites turn onto their backs when they die;
  Dinnerbone players only turn over while they show their cape.

## Updating

Replace the jar in `mods/` and restart the server. Configuration and cameras stay as they are.
