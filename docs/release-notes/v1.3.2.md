Guardian beams, screaming endermen, moving pistons, rowing boats and dripping stalactites. The next part of the
[roadmap](https://github.com/DarienRahl/CCTV_Farbic/blob/v1.3.2/docs/ROADMAP.md) (1.3, a 1:1 picture).

## New

- **Guardians fire their beams** at their targets: the beam charges from purple to yellow like in the
  game, with bubbles along it under water and its rising attack sound. Their spikes draw in while they
  swim and their tails sway like the game's model, and the eye follows its target (or the camera).
- **Endermen scream** when a player stares at them: the jaw drops, they shake and the stare sound
  plays. The block an enderman carries is in its hands, and portal specks drift around endermen and
  endermites; blazes smoke.
- **Pistons move blocks** smoothly like in the game: the pushed and pulled blocks slide, the piston
  head slides out and back (short while it passes through the base).
- **Boats row**: the paddles move while someone rows, and boats rock when they are hit.
- **Stalactites drip** water or lava (whatever is above them; dry ones drip water now and then) and
  full beehives drip honey, with the sound of the drops landing.
- The warden's heartbeat speeds up the angrier it is.

## For developers

- Entity frames carry the boat's rowing times ("rowL", "rowR"), its hurt time, direction and damage and
  the bubble column tilt, and a guardian's beam target ("beam": entity id); "d" carries whether a
  guardian moves or is in water, the warden's anger and whether an enderman was stared at.
- Environment samples say what dry stalactites drip ("drip") and whether water evaporates ("evaporates").
- Blocks moved by pistons travel with the effects ("pm": destination, direction, extending, progress,
  the block, its short piston head form and a retracting piston's base); CI powers a piston and checks it.

## Updating

Replace the jar in `mods/` and restart the server. Configuration and cameras stay as they are.
