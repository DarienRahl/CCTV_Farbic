Glowing outlines, names on framed maps and pieces of items. The next part of the
[roadmap](https://github.com/DarienRahl/CCTV_Farbic/blob/v1.3.4/docs/ROADMAP.md) (1.3, a 1:1 picture).

## New

- **Glowing entities** (the Glowing effect, spectral arrows, `/effect`, the glowing tag) are outlined like in
  the game: in their team's colour, soft edged (the game's own entity_outline post effect ported to the
  viewer) and seen through walls.
- **Maps in item frames** show the names of their markers (named banners) under the icons, like MapRenderer.
- **Pieces of items**: food and potions crumble while players and mobs eat or drink, and tools and armour
  burst into pieces with their break sound when they break. Snowballs, slime balls and cobwebs break into
  pieces too when the server sends those particles.

## For developers

- Entities that glow carry their outline colour ("glow"). New effect "ip": an item and the positions and
  speeds of its pieces, taken where the game makes them (LivingEntity.spawnItemParticles and
  onEquippedItemBroken, through a new optional mixin). CI makes a cow glow and breaks a wolf's armour.

## Updating

Replace the jar in `mods/` and restart the server. Configuration and cameras stay as they are.
