Every layer of every renderer: what mobs hold and wear on top of their models, the warden's glow, vaults' and
ominous item spawners' items, cushions and dragon fireballs.

## New

- **Mooshrooms** carry their mushrooms on their back and head, and **snow golems** wear their carved pumpkin
  until they are sheared.
- **Villagers and wandering traders** hold what they offer in their crossed arms, **witches** raise their
  potion to their nose while they drink, and **dolphins** carry what they picked up in their mouth. Villagers
  who were just refused shake their heads.
- **The warden** glows like in the game: its pulsating spots fade in and out in turns, its tendrils light up
  when it hears something and its heart beats faster the angrier it gets.
- **Vaults** spin the item they show in their cage, and **ominous item spawners** grow and spin their item
  before it drops.
- **Cushions** in their colour and **dragon fireballs** facing the camera.

## Updating

Replace the jar in `mods/` and restart the server. Configuration and cameras stay as they are.
