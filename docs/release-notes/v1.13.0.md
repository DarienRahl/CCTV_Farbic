The game itself as the judge: every CI run now compares the viewer with Minecraft's own pictures of five scenes,
and the gaps they showed are closed.

## New

- **Reference renders of five shots**: the game (a client game test) and the viewer take pictures of the same
  scenes from the same eyes — day, night, a row of mobs with their equipment, a room with block entities and under
  water — and CI scores them side by side with a bound for each. All five are within 0.5 % of the game.

## Fixed

- **Distant textures look like the game's**: the block atlas's mip levels are made like 26.3's MipmapGenerator,
  with each texture's own mipmap strategy (glass and redstone dust averaged, flowers and plants keeping their
  shape, leaves darkening in the distance) and in linear light.
- **Skeletons** only raise their bow when they are about to shoot; otherwise they hold it at their side.

## Updating

Replace the jar in `mods/` and restart the server. Configuration and cameras stay as they are.
