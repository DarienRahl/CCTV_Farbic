Everything that moves or floats: CI now compares the viewer with Minecraft's own pictures of flowing water and lava,
display entities, mannequins, vehicles and projectiles, and the gaps they showed are closed. Camera markers are back
in the world.

## New

- **Seven more reference shots**:
  - water and lava: a waterfall and a lava fall into holes, water flowing down trenches one and three blocks wide,
    lava's three blocks of flow, and blocks full of water (stairs, slabs, a fence, a pane, bars, a lantern, a chest,
    a grate, seagrass, kelp, sea pickles, roots, a coral fan)
  - display entities: blocks turned, stretched and made bigger, items in their display contexts, texts turned to the
    camera, wrapped, with a background, a shadow, see through, both ways round
  - mannequins in their profiles' skins: crouching, left-handed with a sword and a shield, in armour, with their
    outer layers hidden, with an elytra, holding a pickaxe and a torch
  - vehicles (boats, a raft and every kind of minecart), projectiles (arrows, a trident, thrown items, fireworks,
    potions, wind charges), fireballs (fireballs, the dragon's, wither skulls, a shulker bullet, llama spit,
    experience orbs) and blocks (primed TNT in each part of its fuse, falling blocks)

  Thirty-nine shots now, each with a bound in CI.

## Fixed

- **Camera markers**: the small observer block that shows a camera in the world was empty since 26.3, which reads a
  block state as a block's id or as a map of its `id` and `properties` only. Markers placed before get their
  observer back when their chunk loads; the smoke test checks both.
- **Thrown items** show the entity's own item (a potion in its colour, an egg of its kind) as it lies on the ground,
  turned like the camera: three times as big for a ghast's fireball, three quarters for a blaze's, at full light for
  fireballs and eyes of ender.
- **Wind charges** are the game's WindChargeModel, wrapped in wind like the breeze, not a flat item.
- **Shulker bullets** tumble with their age inside a translucent copy of themselves (they spun by the clock, also in a
  frozen world); **wither skulls** turn by their pitch and are blue when dangerous; **llama spit** is turned like
  the game turns it.
- **Experience orbs** are translucent, turned like the camera and lit seven levels brighter than their block.
- **End crystals** are tilted like EndCrystalModel, with their base where the game draws it.
- **Primed TNT** swells about its middle and, in a frozen world, flashes white at the right ticks; TNT, falling
  blocks and experience orbs cast their shadows.
- **Arrows** hide the back of their flat crosses like the game (they were drawn dark).
- **Frozen worlds** (`/tick freeze`): like the game, a display entity is drawn once it has ticked and a mannequin
  wears its profile's skin once it has ticked, so ones that appear while the world is frozen stay hidden or in the
  default skin.

## Updating

Replace the jar in `mods/` and restart the server. Configuration and cameras stay as they are.
